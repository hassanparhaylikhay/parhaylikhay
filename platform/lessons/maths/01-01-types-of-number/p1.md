## Why this matters

Cambridge expects you to read and write numbers the way they do (spaces, not commas; decimals read digit-by-digit) and to know what a reciprocal is. Both are 1-mark questions that show up every year.

## Reading large numbers

Group digits in threes from the right. Use a **space**, not a comma:

- 3 zeros = thousand
- 6 zeros = million
- 9 zeros = billion
- 12 zeros = trillion

So $6\,000\,000\,000$ has 9 zeros, which means **six billion**.

## Writing from words

The word **"and"** is just a connector between groups. It doesn't add zeros.

*Ten thousand and seven* → thousands group = 10, ones group = 007 → **10 007**.

## Decimals in words

Read the whole part normally, say **"point"**, then read each digit **one by one**:

- $3.25$ is *three point two five*
- $0.057$ is *zero point zero five seven*

## Reciprocals

The **reciprocal** of $n$ is $\dfrac{1}{n}$. That's it.

- Whole number: reciprocal of $5$ is $\tfrac{1}{5}$.
- Fraction: **flip it.** Reciprocal of $\tfrac{2}{5}$ is $\tfrac{5}{2}$.
- Negative: sign stays the same. Reciprocal of $-3$ is $-\tfrac{1}{3}$.
- $0$ has **no reciprocal** ($\tfrac{1}{0}$ is undefined).

<div class="trap-callout">

**Trap: reciprocal vs negative.** The reciprocal of $3$ is $\tfrac{1}{3}$, not $-3$. *Negative* flips the sign. *Reciprocal* flips the number. Totally different.

</div>

## Worked example 1

Write $1\,200\,003$ in words.

**Step 1: Group the digits in threes from the right.**

$$1\,\mid\,200\,\mid\,003$$

**Step 2: Read each group with its scale.**

- Millions: $1$ → *one million*
- Thousands: $200$ → *two hundred thousand*
- Ones: $003$ → *three*

**Answer:** *one million two hundred thousand and three*. **[B1]**

Full marks $= 1$.

## Common mistakes

- Writing 10 007 as 10 070 or 100 007 because of the word "and".
- Reading $3.25$ as *"three point twenty-five"*. Digits go one by one.
- Reciprocal of $-3$ written as $\tfrac{1}{3}$. The minus stays.
- Treating $0$ like it has a reciprocal. It doesn't.

## Quick summary

- Group digits in threes with spaces.
- "And" is just a connector.
- Decimals: digits one by one after "point".
- Reciprocal = $\tfrac{1}{n}$. Flip fractions. Sign stays. $0$ has none.
