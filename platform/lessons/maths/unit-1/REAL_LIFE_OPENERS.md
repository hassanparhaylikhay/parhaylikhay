## Voice notes

All openers use plain teacher prose, second-person where natural, British English, no em dashes, no emojis, 1-2 sentences each. Lead-ins were varied across "Imagine…", "Picture…", "Think of…", "Have you ever…", "Try…", and a couple of bare scenarios ("Pick up a ruler and…", "Two mobile data plans:"), so the unit doesn't read like a single hook template repeated 17 times.
