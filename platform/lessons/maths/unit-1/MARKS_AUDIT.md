## Notes on house style observed

- Single-part 1-mark questions consistently use `[B1]`.
- Single-part 2-mark questions consistently use `[M1 for ...; A1 for ...]`.
- Single-part 3-mark questions use `[M1 ...; M1 ...; A1 ...]` or `[M1 ...; A1 ...; A1 ...]` depending on whether the answer naturally splits in two.
- Multi-part questions with each part worth 1 mark consistently use `[B1]` per part (sometimes aggregated as `[B1 each]` at the end for compactness).
- Multi-part questions with mixed mark counts (rare in Unit 1) allocate codes per part.
- The pattern `[B1 + B1 + A1]` appears in 01-07-indices-i p4 WE2 (find $9^{1/2} + 27^{2/3}$) and 01-10-limits-of-accuracy p1 WE (bounds: lower, upper, range). These are acceptable when each component is a genuinely independent calculation.
