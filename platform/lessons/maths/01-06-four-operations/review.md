You've now seen all four operations across all three number types: integers, fractions, and decimals. Plus the BIDMAS rule that ties them together when expressions mix operations.

## The skills you should have

- **Sign rules** for $+$, $-$, $\times$, $\div$ on negative numbers.
- **Adding/subtracting fractions** with a common denominator.
- **Multiplying/dividing fractions** (× straight across; ÷ flips and multiplies).
- **Mixed numbers** converted to improper before any operation.
- **Decimal arithmetic**: align points for ± , count places for ×, shift for ÷.
- **BIDMAS** order of operations, including the equal-level left-to-right rule.

| Mixed-operation expression | 2 | M1 BIDMAS method, A1 correct answer |

## The single biggest mistake

**Skipping the order**. Students see $3 + 4 \times 5$ and write $35$ because they read left to right. Multiplication first, always. If you're not sure, write the expression on a fresh line and replace one sub-expression at a time.
