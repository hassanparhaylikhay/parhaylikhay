You've now seen the two skills in this topic: money arithmetic and currency conversion. Cambridge wraps these into word problems about shopping, salaries, profit, and travel.

## The skills you should have

- **Money to 2 d.p.** always (\$5.00, \$0.40, \$1.50).
- **Add/subtract** with aligned decimal points.
- **Multiply** for totals, **divide** for shares.
- **Profit** = selling price − cost price.
- **Currency conversion**: multiply by the rate to go in its direction, divide to go against.
- **Chained conversions**: keep full precision, round only at the end.
- **Commission**: apply as a percentage on the right amount.

## Marking patterns

| Question style | Marks | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| "Find the total cost" | 1 | B1 for the answer in dollars |
| "How much change" | 1 | B1 for the difference, in 2-d.p. form |
| "Find the earnings" | 2 | M1 for the time conversion (if mixed-unit), A1 for the answer |
| "Convert X USD to PKR" | 1–2 | M1 for the rate direction, A1 for the answer |
| "Find the profit per item / total" | 2 | M1 for the per-item profit, A1 for the total |

## The single biggest mistake

**Money written without 2 d.p.** "\$5" or "\$3.5" or "\$0.4" all lose the accuracy mark. The correct forms are \$5.00, \$3.50, \$0.40. This is a Cambridge convention; ignore it and you give up easy marks.
