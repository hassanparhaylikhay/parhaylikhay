You've now seen the three pillars of time: unit conversion, clock formats, and timetables / zones. Cambridge mixes these freely, so practice across all three.

## The skills you should have

- **Conversions** between seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years.
- **12-hour and 24-hour clock** conversion in both directions.
- **Elapsed time** using the chunk method (or the two-chunk method across midnight).
- **Adding a duration** to a start time, with carry from minutes to hours.
- **Time zones**: ahead = add, behind = subtract; watch for date crossing.

## The single biggest mistake

**Treating time as decimal.** $2$ h $30$ min is $2.5$ h, not $2.30$ h. $30$ min $\div 60 = 0.5$ h. Same logic applies whenever you switch between mixed-unit ($h$ and $min$) and decimal ($h$).
