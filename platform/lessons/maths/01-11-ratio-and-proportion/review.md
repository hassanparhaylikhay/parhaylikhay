You've now seen the three skills in this topic: simplifying ratios, dividing a quantity in a given ratio, and proportional reasoning across recipes, maps, and best-value problems. Cambridge mixes these freely and almost always wraps them in real-world context.

## The skills you should have

- **Simplifying** ratios using the HCF, including ratios with decimals, fractions, or different units.
- **Dividing** a quantity by adding the parts, finding one part, then multiplying for each share.
- **Working backwards** from a single share to the total.
- **Proportional reasoning** by finding the value of one unit, then multiplying.
- **Map scales** including converting cm to m or km.
- **Best value** by comparing cost per unit.

## The single biggest mistake

**Dividing the total by one of the ratio numbers instead of the SUM.** "Share \$60 in $2 : 3$": divide by $5$ (sum of parts), not by $2$ or $3$. This single error costs students a full mark every year.
