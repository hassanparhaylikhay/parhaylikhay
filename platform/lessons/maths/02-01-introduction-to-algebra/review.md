## What you should know by now

If you've worked through P1 to P3 you can:

- Read algebra correctly: $3a$ is multiplication, $a^2$ is a square, $ab$ is a product.
- Identify the **coefficient** of any letter and decide whether two terms are **like** or **unlike**.
- Simplify by combining like terms.
- Substitute values into expressions and formulas, including negative values and powers.
- Apply **BIDMAS** when evaluating after substitution.
- Recognise common Cambridge formulas (motion, area, volume) and substitute carefully.

## Marking patterns at a glance

This sub-topic is rarely a single big question. It surfaces inside other questions: the first step of a longer problem usually involves substituting a value or simplifying. The marks are:

- **B1** for a single-step substitution that gives the right value (e.g., "find $v$ when $u, a, t$ are given").
- **M1 + A1** if there's a clear method step (e.g., "show your working") and a final accuracy mark.
- **B1 (B1)** for two-part questions where each substitution earns one mark.

The trap markers Cambridge uses repeatedly:

- Negative values inside a power. Always wrap in brackets.
- "In terms of $\pi$": don't decimalise.
- Multi-letter formulas like $s = ut + \tfrac{1}{2}at^2$: work each term separately.

## Worked example: a multi-step substitution

The kinetic energy formula is $E = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2$, where $m$ is mass and $v$ is velocity. Find $E$ when $m = 4$ and $v = 6$.

**Step 1: Square the velocity first.**

$$v^2 \;=\; (6)^2 \;=\; 36$$

**Step 2: Multiply by the mass.**

$$mv^2 \;=\; (4)(36) \;=\; 144$$

**Step 3: Halve.**

$$E \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}(144) \;=\; 72$$

**Answer:** $E = \boxed{72}$. **[M1 for substitution; A1 for the value]**

Full marks $= 2$.

## Quick reference

- $3a = 3 \times a$, $ab = a \times b$, $a^2 = a \times a$.
- Coefficient is the number in front (default $1$).
- Like terms have the same letter and the same power.
- Substituted values go in **brackets**, especially negatives.
- BIDMAS applies: indices before multiplication before addition.
