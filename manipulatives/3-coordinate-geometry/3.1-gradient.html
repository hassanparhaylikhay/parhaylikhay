m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)
Cambridge tip: write m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁) and substitute — that earns M1 even if the arithmetic goes wrong. Negative m = slopes down left to right. Horizontal line: m = 0. Vertical line: m is undefined.
