# Plan: Quadratic Graphs

## Cambridge Syllabus Reference
C2.5 — Drawing and interpreting graphs of quadratic functions  
y = ax² + bx + c (where a ≠ 0)

## Common Student Misconceptions
1. Thinking 'b' simply shifts the graph horizontally — it shifts the **axis of symmetry**, not the whole curve
2. Confusing roots (x-intercepts) with the y-intercept
3. Forgetting to check the sign of 'a' before deciding which way the parabola opens
4. Not writing the factorised form in working — loses the M1 method mark
5. Sign errors in factorisation: e.g. writing (x + 2)(x + 3) for x² - 5x + 6

## Past Paper Frequency
Appears in ~85% of Paper 2 exams. One of the most reliable topics to score full marks on.

## Mark Scheme Patterns
- B1 for correct y-intercept identified
- M1 for correct factorisation method shown
- A1 for each correct root
- B1 for correct parabola shape (U or ∩)
- B1 for vertex or turning point with correct coordinates
